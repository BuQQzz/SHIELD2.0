# SHIELD v0.3.0 announcement: X thread

Post when the v0.3.0 release is published (post 1 says "is out", post 8 offers the download). Every post is under 280 characters.

Media ideas: a short clip of Teams working, or of Connect → sign in → a tool call (posts 2 and 5).

---

**1/**

SHIELD v0.3 is out: our biggest update yet. A near-complete overhaul of how it runs local models, plus connectors to your tools.

A private AI agent on your own PC. No cloud, no telemetry. 🧵

**2/**

👥 Teams

Give SHIELD a big task and an orchestrator splits it across sub-agents: a coder writes, a reviewer checks. On one 8 GB GPU.

Agents hand off with a short report, and one that runs out of room picks up from its handoff instead of starting over.

**3/**

🧠 Recall

Before each message, SHIELD searches your past conversations and hands the model the one or two that matter. No extra model, no cloud.

It even flags past "fixes" that were never actually made, so old mistakes don't come back as facts.

**4/**

📒 It remembers what really happened

SHIELD keeps its own record of what a session changed and ran, apart from what the model says it did. Projects keep their folder and AGENTS.md instructions across restarts.

**5/**

🔌 Connectors (new)

Press Connect, sign in in your browser, done: Notion, Linear, Jira & Confluence, Asana, Sentry, Zapier, Hugging Face, Context7, GitHub.

Or add any MCP server yourself. Tools reach the model as native calls, sign-ins stay encrypted.

**6/**

🛡️ Safety built in, not bolted on

Every action goes through your permission mode. Prompts say when a call leaves your project or is risky. Web pages and file contents are treated as untrusted, so instructions hidden in them aren't obeyed.

**7/**

⚡ Under the hood

Bonsai 2 27B with a 32K context on an 8 GB card. Native tool calls everywhere, team agents included. Smarter context for long sessions: outlines of big files, fewer slow re-reads, summaries that keep your requests.
